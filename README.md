# Finance Strategy & Operations – Cryst finance (2016–2019)

Demonstrates my role at Cryst finance, a private finance company providing Gold, Business, and Home loans, as well as Travel and Money Transfer services. Includes dashboards, SOPs, SQL insights, and process documentation.

## 🧰 Tools & Skills
- Excel & Power BI – Customer portfolio, risk tracking, repayment forecasts  
- SQL – Payment behavior and loan performance analysis  
- Word – SOPs for loan processing, travel services, and compliance  
- PowerPoint – Monthly performance and audit presentations

## 📊 Key Contributions
- Built a gold loan portfolio tracking dashboard to monitor NPA trends  
- Analyzed repayment patterns to identify high-risk clients via SQL insights  
- Introduced SOPs to standardize loan, travel, and remittance processes  
- Supported compliance and audit workflows with monthly reporting  
- Streamlined onboarding and travel logistics using checklists and templates
