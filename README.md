# Finance Strategy & Operations – Crystal Investments (2016–2019)

This project demonstrates my work at Crystal Investments, a private finance company, where I supported operational strategy, digital transformation, and stakeholder reporting. My role as an Administrative Manager involved aligning business objectives with execution efficiency in a high-compliance financial environment.

## 🧰 Tools & Skills
- Excel & Power BI – KPI dashboards, client portfolio tracking
- SQL – Customer repayment pattern analysis
- Process Mapping – SOPs for loan processing and client onboarding
- Client Documentation & Service Audits
- Cross-functional Coordination & Performance Reporting

## 📊 Key Contributions
- Designed and implemented a client lifecycle tracking system using Excel and SQL
- Optimized internal workflows and document management processes
- Introduced service quality KPIs and created reporting templates for leadership
- Improved operational turnaround time by 15% through standardization and digital tools
- Supported compliance reporting and internal audits
